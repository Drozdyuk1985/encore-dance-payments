The problem: Zelle money with no memory

  • Group semesters are paid online (Wix). The rest, $160 privates, $70 drop-ins, packages and the $500/week plan, comes by Zelle after class, when people remember
  • Today: Apple Notes "Running schedule" + a "To Do" line like "Alex $160", updated by hand from Zelle emails
  • The bank shows a name and an amount, with no link to the lesson. It's often a spouse, a parent or a company paying
  • The "system" is the owner's memory at 11 pm, after teaching all day

Encore reads Zelle emails and the Chase export as is, keeps only incoming Zelle, learns each payer's rhythm, computes balances in code, and lets AI do only judgment and language. Fits Apple Notes (paste the schedule or "who owes" list) and tracks prepaid packages plus a $500/week unlimited plan. Private by design: the AI sees IDs, not names (except an odd-format email the Email Reader must read).

Safety: it checks, and can't be talked into paying you

  • Injection: code pre-screen (0 tokens) → untrusted wrapper → provider filter → no "mark paid" tool
  • Invented facts: amounts only from code; typed $ in drafts are rejected
  • Wrong action: nothing sent without the owner; no threats; tone escalates slowly
  • Fake Zelle emails blocked; a payment in an email and the bank counts once
  • Privacy: non-Zelle rows dropped; AI sees C007, not names (exception: odd-format emails)
  • Overspend: strict 400k tokens/day cap, 6 tool steps, 20 calls/min

  • Timed, same week: ≈5 min the usual way → ≈2 min with Encore; 0 owed in both, none missed
