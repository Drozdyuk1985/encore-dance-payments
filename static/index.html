Import your Zelle payments

Two ways in. Zelle emails are fastest: a payment counts the same evening. The Chase CSV is the full record. Use both. A payment seen in an email and later in the bank file is counted once.

1 · Zelle emails

Gmail: open a "You received money with Zelle®" email → ⋮ → Download message (.eml), then choose the files here. Or select the email text and paste it below (several emails are fine; put a line of dashes between them). Apple Mail: drag the emails onto the file picker. Standard alerts are read by code; only unusual formats go to the Email Reader agent, and its answer counts only if the name and amount appear word for word in the email. Fake Zelle emails ("pending", "upgrade your account", odd sender) are flagged and never counted.

2 · Chase CSV export

In Chase: open the business checking account → Download account activity → choose CSV and a date range. Encore keeps only incoming Zelle payments (date, sender, amount). Every other row, like payroll, taxes, loans and card payments, is thrown away right away and never stored or shown to the AI.

3 · Cash or check

Paid in person? Record it here so the balance is right. Cash and checks aren't in the Zelle data, so they can't be counted twice.
